# Commercial Use

The Try Again License allows broad use for low-consequence, retry-safe systems.

A commercial license is required when the protected material is used in systems where mistakes are expensive, difficult to reverse, externally consequential, or high-risk.

**The basic rule:**

> If "try again" is an acceptable recovery strategy, Community Use likely applies.
> If "try again" is **not** an acceptable recovery strategy, Commercial Use likely applies.

## Growth / Limited Commercial Thresholds

Some systems may begin as low-risk or human-reviewed but become commercially significant at scale.

A commercial license may be required once any of the following thresholds are exceeded:

- **$1,000,000** in annual recurring revenue
- **$1,000,000** in annual transaction volume
- **100,000** end-user actions per year
- **10,000** customer-facing autonomous interactions per year
- **$100,000** in annual recurring revenue for voice agents, outbound agents, appointment-setting agents, or customer-facing autonomous agents
- Use by an enterprise customer in a production workflow where the system becomes operationally material
- Use in a workflow where failure could create material customer, reputational, compliance, legal, financial, or operational risk

## Autonomous External Action

A commercial license is required if the system takes, recommends, authorizes, routes, verifies, governs, or executes autonomous external action on behalf of a user, company, customer, patient, investor, institution, government, or other third party in a high-consequence context.

Examples include:

- Moving money
- Placing trades
- Executing payments
- Sending legally significant messages
- Sending outbound calls at scale
- Sending customer-facing communications at scale
- Making healthcare recommendations
- Updating patient records
- Updating financial records
- Controlling drones
- Controlling robots
- Initiating procurement
- Approving claims
- Making compliance decisions
- Making eligibility decisions
- Triggering operational workflows
- Changing user access
- Filing legal documents
